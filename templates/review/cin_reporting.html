Observed is the share of pairs that gave the same answer; expected is the share two coders would match by chance, choosing with the frequencies these coders used; kappa is how far observed sits above chance, from 0 (chance) to 1 (always the same). V1 measured 0.583. A rejection counts as one more answer. Kappa depends on the mix of stories drawn, so compare it between rounds drawn the same way.

Over the first two answers on each story ({{ first_pairs }} pairs), so a third coder brought in on a split is not marked down for it. Read a coder's kappa against "Kappa without them", the kappa of the pairs they are not in.

Flagged where a coder's kappa is at least 0.20 below the kappa without them (one Landis–Koch band) and a 95% bootstrap interval of that gap excludes zero: overall from 20 pairs, per category from 10 pairs naming it. A category's use is flagged where a coder chooses it at twice everyone else's rate or under half of it, with p < 0.05, from 20 answers.
