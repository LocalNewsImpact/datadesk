Bylines

Not every byline: the strings a parser produced that look wrong. A near-duplicate spelling of a name already in the dataset, a job title riding along after a name, a publication name, a contact address, or a string that names no person at all. {{ remaining }} to decide.

{% comment %} Co-authors are deliberately absent. "Alyssa Mueller, Marcus Off" is two reporters on one story, which is normal practice and not a parse error -- the reports below count it as two people without anybody deciding anything. {% endcomment %}

A decision is recorded once per string and applied to every article carrying it, including articles extracted later. See the reports.
